What you're paying for

Five layers, top to bottom.

A watch is a stack of parts, and the price is decided by which ones you skimp on. Here is every layer in a NERVA, and what a cheaper watch usually substitutes.

  • 01 · Glass
    Sapphire crystal

    Scratches only against harder stone. Cheaper watches use mineral glass, which scuffs in a month against a doorframe.

  • 02 · Case
    316L stainless steel

    The surgical grade. It doesn't turn a green ring on your wrist in Cairo summer the way plated brass does.

  • 03 · Movement
    Miyota or Seiko, named

    Japanese movements, listed by reference on every product. Any watchmaker can service them and parts are easy to find.

  • 04 · Seal
    Screw-down caseback

    Rated and tested, not decorative. The Deepline is pressure-tested to 200m; the dress models are 30m, and we say so.

  • 05 · Strap
    Quick-release, standard width

    20mm and 18mm, changeable by hand in seconds. No proprietary fitting that forces you back to us.
