Questions

Before you book.

Do I need camping or desert experience?
No. Most people on our trips have never camped before. The guide handles the tent, the fire and the route — you handle showing up and being reasonably fit for walking.
What's actually included?
Transport from the Cairo pickup point, the guide, camping equipment, and every meal from the first lunch to the last breakfast. Not included: alcohol, and anything personal like extra snacks.
What if the group doesn't fill up?
We run with a minimum of four. If it doesn't reach that by a week out, you get a full refund or the option to move to the next date — your choice.
Is it safe to travel alone and join a group?
Most people book solo. Groups of six with one guide mean you're never actually alone, and roughly half our travellers come without a companion.
What's the cancellation policy?
Full refund up to two weeks before departure. Inside two weeks, the deposit isn't refundable but can be moved to a different date once.
