The six

Who they are, in one paragraph each.

Mercedes-Benz

Stuttgart · 1926

The oldest carmaker still trading, formed when Daimler and Benz merged. Historically the one that introduced a safety idea first and let everyone else copy it a decade later.

Known for: engineering that arrives early

BMW

Munich · 1916

Aircraft engines first, motorcycles second, cars third. Nearly bought by Mercedes in 1959 before a saloon aimed at drivers rather than passengers saved it.

Known for: rear drive and straight sixes

Porsche

Stuttgart · 1931

Began as a consultancy designing cars for other companies, including the original Volkswagen. Has spent sixty years refining one rear-engined shape that theory says shouldn't work.

Known for: the engine in the wrong place

Audi

Ingolstadt · 1909

Four rings for four merged companies. Spent decades as the quiet one until permanent four-wheel drive on a road car rewrote what a rally car was.

Known for: quattro, and interiors

Volkswagen

Wolfsburg · 1937

Built to put Germany on wheels, then rescued after the war by a British army officer. Has twice made the definitive version of a car type everyone else then imitated.

Known for: getting the format right

Opel

Rüsselsheim · 1862

Sewing machines, then bicycles, then cars. General Motors' European arm for ninety years, and the one that made technology affordable before the others did.

Known for: mass-market firsts
