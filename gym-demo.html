The Philosophy

Trained with
intent.

AXIOM was founded on a simple premise: the difference between exercising and training is a plan someone is accountable for. Every member begins with a full movement screen and a programme written by name — not a template, not a class of forty.

Our coaches carry the load of the details: tempo, load progression, sleep, recovery windows, the honest conversation when a week goes sideways. You bring the hour. We make certain it counts — and we measure it, quarter after quarter, so progress is something you can read rather than hope for.

01

Precision Programming

Every block is periodised against your screen data, training age, and calendar — then revised every fourth week.

02

Personalised Coaching

One coach who knows your history, your injuries, and the lift you keep avoiding. Continuity is the whole method.

03

Results You Can Read

Quarterly assessments across strength, composition, and capacity. Documented, compared, and reviewed with you.

Programs

Four routes.
One standard.

Members move between formats as their season demands — a build phase, a return from injury, a race on the calendar. The programming follows you across all of them.

Strength & Conditioning

Barbell-led periodisation for absolute strength, built on squat, hinge, press and carry. Progressive overload managed to the kilo.

1-on-1 Coaching

Recovery & Mobility
