The F30.

The 3 Series that went turbo across the whole range, grew a 2 810 mm wheelbase, and traded hydraulic steering for electric — the generation where the compact BMW became a modern car.

Scroll to walk around it
01 · Engine bay All turbocharged

Every engine, forced induction.

The F30 is the generation that retired the naturally aspirated six from the volume cars. A twin-scroll four does the work in the 320i and 328i; the straight-six survives, turbocharged, in the 335i. Pick one — the specs follow.

TwinPower Turbo. BMW's label for the whole package rather than the turbo count: one twin-scroll turbocharger, direct injection, Double-VANOS and Valvetronic throttle-less load control working together. The 328i's peak torque arrives at 1 250 rpm and holds to 4 800.

02 · Inside Freestanding display

The screen leaves the dashboard.

iDrive's display stops being a slot in the fascia and stands proud of it — the arrangement every BMW since has kept. The driver's side of the cabin still angles toward the seat, just far less than an E30's did.

Trim lines instead of one interior. Rather than a single cabin with an options list, the F30 shipped as Sport, Luxury or Modern Line — each with its own materials, stitching and grille finish — with M Sport joining part-way through 2012.

03 · Chassis Electric steering

The generation that went electric at the wheel.

Electric power steering became standard — it frees the engine from driving a pump, and it is the change owners argue about most. Underneath, a double-joint strut front axle and a five-link rear carry over the geometry the E90 established.

Wheelbase up 50 mm. At 2 810 mm between the axles and 4 624 mm overall, the F30 is a size up on the E90 — most of it spent on rear legroom, and the reason this is the first 3 Series that stopped feeling small in the back.

04 · Load bay Saloon & Touring

480 litres — and the year the coupé left.

The saloon arrived first in February 2012, the Touring that autumn. What did not arrive was a coupé: from this generation on it became the 4 Series, and the 3 Series badge stayed on cars with four doors.

EfficientDynamics as standard. Auto start-stop, brake energy regeneration, an electric water pump and an ECO PRO mode that will coast with the driveline disengaged — fitted across the range, not sold as a green special.

05 · Pick one

The one that had to carry everything.

Sold as a saloon, a Touring, a GT and a long-wheelbase car, built on four continents, and asked to be both the efficient one and the quick one. Most of what a 3 Series is today was decided here.
