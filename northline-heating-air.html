Questions

The things people ask us most

Do you really charge the same after hours?
Yes. Nights, weekends and holidays are billed at the exact same flat rate as a Tuesday morning. A furnace doesn't check the clock before it fails, so we don't either.
How fast can someone actually get here?
Our average arrival is about two hours for no-heat and no-cool emergencies. For non-urgent work you'll usually get a slot within 48 hours, and you always pick a two-hour window rather than waiting all day.
What does the $89 diagnostic actually cover?
A full system inspection, a written flat-rate quote for whatever we find, and photos of the problem so you're not taking our word for it. If you approve the repair, the $89 comes straight off the bill.
Will you try to sell me a new system?
Only if repair genuinely doesn't make financial sense, and we'll show you the math. Our techs are paid hourly, not on commission, so nobody here earns more by selling you equipment you don't need.
Do you offer financing?
Yes, on replacements and larger repairs — from $84/month with $0 down on approved credit. We'll also handle the utility rebate paperwork for qualifying high-efficiency systems.
