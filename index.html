Questions

The things people ask first.

How long does it really take?
Two to four weeks for most sites, and the date is agreed before work starts. The part that slows projects down is almost never the building — it's waiting on text and photographs. If you have those ready, it's quicker.
What do I need to give you?
Whatever you already have: logo, photos, existing text, prices. If some of it doesn't exist yet, I'll write placeholder copy so the build isn't held up, and we replace it before launch. You don't need a brand guide.
Do I own the site afterwards?
Yes, entirely. You get the files, the domain stays in your name, and the site isn't locked to a platform that charges you monthly to keep it running. If you decide to work with someone else later, they can pick it up without a rebuild.
What if I need changes later?
Small edits — prices, opening hours, a new photo — you can make yourself after the handover. For anything bigger, I quote per job. There's no retainer and no monthly fee unless you specifically want one.
Do you handle hosting and domains?
I set both up and hand over the logins. They're billed at cost, usually around 2,000 EGP a year in total. I don't mark them up or resell them, so you're never locked in.
Why does the pre-flight report matter?
Plenty of sites look right on a designer's laptop and show a blank screen on a three-year-old phone, because the content waits for JavaScript to reveal it. That's a real failure I've had to fix, so every site is now tested with scripts switched off before launch.
