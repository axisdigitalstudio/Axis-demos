Production

Four machines,
eight product lines.

Different substrates need different presses. A desk mat is a textile problem; an acrylic sign is a rigid-surface problem. We run both rather than compromising on one.

01 — UV

Flatbed UV printing

Ink laid directly onto acrylic, wood and rigid board, then cured under UV in the same pass. Scratch-resistant, vivid, and dry the moment it leaves the bed.

02 — Dye-sub

Sublimation & high-DPI textile

Heat turns the dye to gas so it enters the fibre instead of coating it. Gradients stay smooth across a 90cm mat and the surface keeps its glide.

03 — Laser

Precision laser cutting

Polished, flame-clear edges on acrylic shapes, wall panels and nameplates. Cut to your outline, not to a stock rectangle.

04 — Die-cut

Precision die-cut vinyl

Industrial plotting for sticker sheets and phone skins. Kiss-cut through the vinyl only, so stickers lift cleanly and the backing stays whole.

From file to doorstep

Four steps. You are involved in two of them.

Step 01

Order & upload

Pick the product and the size, then send the file — PNG, JPG, PDF or a vector. If the resolution is too low for the size you picked, we tell you before you pay.

Same day
Step 02

Digital preview & proof

You get a mock-up of your exact piece — real dimensions, real bleed, real crop. Change it as many times as you need. Nothing prints without your yes.

Within 24 hours
Step 03

Manufacturing & QC

Printed, cut, and then checked under angled light for banding, dust inclusions and edge chips. Anything that fails gets re-run, not shipped.

1–2 working days
Step 04

Packing & shipping

Corner-protected, film-faced and rigid-boxed, then handed to Bosta or Aramex. Cash on delivery across Egypt, with tracking from the moment it leaves.

3–5 days nationwide
